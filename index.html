Getting there

How to reach Khajuraho.

Khajuraho is a small, easy-going temple town. Here's how to get to the venue.

✦

By air

Khajuraho Airport (HJR) is about 20 minutes by taxi from the venue.

Direct flights are limited, mostly from Delhi, so book early.

By train

Khajuraho station (KURJ) is about 20–25 minutes by taxi from the venue, with direct trains from Delhi and a few other cities.

Otherwise, Jhansi (about 4 hrs by road) and Satna (about 3 hrs) are the nearest big junctions.

By road

Khajuraho is about 45 km from Chhatarpur. It's about 4 hrs from Jhansi and 6 hrs from Gwalior.

The venue is on Bamitha Road. Search for "A S Hotels Khajuraho" in Google Maps.

Getting around

Taxis and autos are easy to find at the airport and station.
